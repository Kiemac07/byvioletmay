# ByVioletMay Website

A responsive multi-page website for ByVioletMay, designed around the existing logo and editorial makeup-brand feel.

## Pages
- `index.html` — Home
- `about.html` — About Violet
- `services.html` — Services & pricing
- `gallery.html` — Portfolio
- `book.html` — Booking page / future booking-system hand-off

## Media
All supplied portfolio photos are in `assets/`.
The two supplied videos are also in `assets/`, with poster images for their thumbnail cards.

### Photo behaviour
Every displayed portfolio/client photo is clickable. It opens in a full-size lightbox with an X button at the top-right.

### Video behaviour
The portfolio videos show a plain triangle play icon. Clicking opens the video viewer, starts playback and attempts to enter fullscreen. Videos do not loop and automatically close the viewer when playback finishes. The X button can also close them manually.

## Design
Warm ivory, espresso brown and muted cocoa tones, with Cormorant Garamond for the editorial serif and DM Sans for supporting text.

## Future booking system
The booking page is intentionally isolated so a platform such as the one selected later can be integrated without redesigning the rest of the site.
